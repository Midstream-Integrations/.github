# Code of conduct

Everyone taking part in Midstream Integrations projects is expected to follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Be respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work. Report unacceptable behavior to **intro@MidstreamIntegrations.com**. Reports are handled confidentially.
